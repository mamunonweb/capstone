\begin{center}
    \section*{Abstract}
\end{center}
Finding potato leaf diseases early and correctly is important for food security and helps farmers lose less money. Deep Learning (DL) models can detect diseases from leaf photos. But many of these models are large, run slowly on cheap devices and are hard to trust, because they work like a ``black box''. In this study we build a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) with Global Average Pooling and a lightweight classification head (2,264,389 parameters, 8.64~MB of model weights). We compare it with MultiNet-V3 Ensemble (EfficientNetB0, MobileNetV2 and ResNet50 combined) and InceptionV3 on our curated outdoor potato leaf dataset. The dataset has 2,321 field images in 5 classes (Bacteria, Fungi, Healthy, Phytophthora and Virus). We split it into training, validation and test sets (80:10:10, giving 1,859 train, 231 validation and 231 test images) with a leak-aware grouped split. All models were trained in Kaggle notebooks on dual NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPUs with ImageNet pre-trained weights and class weighting. Under these conditions, our proposed MobileNetV2 got the highest test accuracy, 93.51\% (macro-F1 0.9272, test loss 0.4466). MultiNet-V3 Ensemble got 90.91\% (macro-F1 0.8975) and InceptionV3 got 84.42\% (macro-F1 0.8296). Our proposed MobileNetV2 was also the smallest model (8.64~MB), while MultiNet-V3 was the largest (118.55~MB) and InceptionV3 took 85.18~MB. We added Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) from the final convolutional layer (\texttt{Conv\_1}) to confirm that the model focuses on leaf lesions instead of soil or plastic mulch, and a Large Language Model (Google Gemini) that turns the predicted class and confidence into simple English and Bengali advice inside our live web application. The results show that a well-tuned small model can beat much larger models on real field photos and is ready for practical use.

\textit{\textbf{Keywords:} Deep Learning; Potato Leaf Disease Detection; Lightweight CNN; Explainable AI; Grad-CAM; Large Language Model; Smart Agriculture.}
